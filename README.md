# YahBible

**A Bible, Scripture, Word &amp; Verse study app — study the Word as it was spoken, verse by verse, tongue by tongue.**

YahBible is a free Windows app for deep study and comparison of the Bible and the other sacred
literatures: a clean reader, 120+ versions side by side, a Hebrew / Greek / Aramaic word engine
with Strong's numbers, interlinear and definitions, the words of Yeshua the Christ in red, and the
revelation of the Name (YHWH / Yahweh Tsidkenu). A grounded AI and video‑critique tools are in
development.

## Download

**&#128229; [Download YahBible.exe](https://huggingface.co/OhBeOneKeyNoBe/YahBible/resolve/main/YahBible.exe)**

1. Download `YahBible.exe`.
2. Double‑click it.
3. It opens in your browser. That's it.

First run fetches the study library; after that it works offline. No account needed to read and
study. (Windows SmartScreen may prompt for a new publisher &mdash; **More info &rarr; Run anyway**.)

## Features

- **Read &amp; compare** — the Bible plus other sacred texts, 120+ versions stacked verse‑for‑verse.
- **Word study** — any word in **Hebrew, Greek &amp; Aramaic**: transliteration, letter meanings, gematria.
- **Strong's, interlinear &amp; definitions** — trace each word to its original and its lexicon entry.
- **Red Letter Words** — the words of Christ (Gospels + Revelation) in the KJV, in scarlet.
- **The Logos** — the revelation of the Name behind every "LORD" in the text.
- **Study panes** — compare several passages and sources side by side.
- *In development:* **Ask Tav'iel** (grounded AI) and **YouTube / TikTok critique**.
